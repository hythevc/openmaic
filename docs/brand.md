# Syllivo brand direction

Working brand selected on 6 November 2026. Syllivo blends the associations of a
syllabus and lively, active learning. Pronounce it **sil-EE-vo**.

## Positioning

**Syllivo turns knowledge into interactive learning.**

Start with professionals who need to understand a new topic, make sense of a
document, or explain something to a team. Syllivo turns a topic or source
material into a guided lesson with explanations, questions, and interactive
activities. AI tutors support the learning experience.

Primary audiences are independent professionals, team leads, trainers, and
knowledge workers. Keep the language useful to an individual learner before
introducing team administration or institution-specific features.

**Tagline:** Make knowledge click.

Suggested supporting copy: “Turn a topic or your own materials into an
interactive lesson. Explore ideas, ask questions, and learn by doing.”

## Voice and experience

- Clear and encouraging, with concrete learning outcomes.
- Professional and approachable; avoid school-themed visual clutter.
- Lead with the next useful action: describe a topic, add materials, or resume
  learning.
- Describe supported capabilities plainly. Do not claim customer counts,
  certifications, measured learning gains, or unavailable paid plans.
- Reveal model and provider configuration when it is needed to complete a task.

## Visual system

| Role | Color | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ivory | `#f8f8f3` | Main canvas; warm, quiet background |
| Ink | `#17231d` | Primary text, headings, navigation |
| Forest | `#244c38` | Primary actions, emphasis, selected states |
| Chartreuse | `#dafa70` | Small accents, highlights, decorative surfaces |

Use an editorial serif for large headings and Inter for interface text, forms,
and navigation. Pair generous space with clear borders and restrained surface
contrast. Chartreuse needs dark text; avoid using it for small text on ivory.
Keep visible keyboard focus and readable mobile layouts.

## Preliminary name screening

These are preliminary live-web observations, not trademark clearance. Searches
were run through Exa and domain availability through Vercel's domain search API
on **6 November 2026**. No domain was purchased.

Exact-name searches for **Syllivo** returned no obvious existing education or
software business in the inspected results. Results included a Greek botanical
[tag page](https://enromiosini.gr/tag/syllivo/) and a
[business name generator](https://www.namestation.com/names/three-syllable-names),
which do not establish an edtech brand collision. An absence in these results is
not proof that the name is unused.
